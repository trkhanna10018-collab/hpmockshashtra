Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 03 October 2026

1. Payment Verification

Payments made through the available payment methods are manually verified by HPMockShashtra.

After making the payment, the user is required to submit the necessary payment details or payment proof through the designated WhatsApp or official support channel.

Access to the purchased product is activated only after the payment has been successfully verified.

Under normal circumstances, the purchased access will be activated within approximately 10 minutes after the required payment details are received and successfully verified.

Since the payment verification process is manual, occasional delays may occur due to payment confirmation, technical issues or other operational reasons.

2. Delay in Access Activation

If the purchased access has not been activated within 1–2 hours after the required payment details have been submitted, the user may contact HPMockShashtra through the official support channel and raise an access or refund request.

The user may be required to provide:

  • Registered name and email address
  • Payment screenshot or transaction details
  • Purchased product details
  • Any other information reasonably required to verify the payment and access status

A refund request raised due to delayed activation will be reviewed after verification of the payment, access activation status and relevant account records.

A delay beyond 1–2 hours does not automatically guarantee a refund. HPMockShashtra may first attempt to activate or restore the purchased access.

3. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Payment has been successfully received but the purchased access has not been provided within a reasonable time.
  • HPMockShashtra is unable to provide the purchased digital service due to an issue attributable to HPMockShashtra.
  • The same payment has been made more than once for the same product.
  • A genuine technical issue prevents the user from accessing the purchased service and the issue cannot reasonably be resolved.
  • HPMockShashtra is otherwise unable to provide the purchased service for which payment has been received.

All refund requests are subject to payment verification and review of the relevant transaction and access records.

4. Refund After Access or Test Attempt

Since HPMockShashtra provides digital services, refunds will generally not be available solely because the user changes their mind after accessing or using the purchased service.

If the purchased mock test or digital content has been:

  • accessed,
  • started,
  • attempted,
  • completed,
  • partially used, or
  • otherwise utilized,

a refund will generally not be available solely on the ground that the access was activated later than the normal activation time.

If a user experiences a delay in access activation, the user is encouraged to report the issue before using the purchased service wherever reasonably possible.

For any delay-related refund request, HPMockShashtra may verify the payment time, payment-details submission time, access activation time and test/access/attempt records before making a decision.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be provided in the following situations:

  • The user has already accessed or attempted the purchased mock test or digital content.
  • The user has used any part of the purchased service during its validity period.
  • The user changes their mind after access has been activated.
  • The user no longer requires the service or decides not to continue their preparation.
  • The user purchases the wrong product and the purchased access has already been activated or used.
  • The user does not use the purchased service during its validity period.
  • The user fails to use the service due to personal reasons after access has been successfully provided.

This section does not prevent consideration of a refund where the service could not be provided due to a verified issue attributable to HPMockShashtra or where otherwise required by applicable law.

6. Technical Problems

If a user experiences a genuine technical problem after payment, HPMockShashtra will first attempt to resolve the issue and restore the purchased access.

If the problem cannot reasonably be resolved and the purchased service cannot be provided, the user may be eligible for a refund after verification.

Users should report technical problems as soon as reasonably possible through the official support channel.

7. Duplicate Payments

If a user accidentally makes more than one payment for the same product, the duplicate transaction may be eligible for a refund after successful verification.

The user may be required to provide the relevant transaction details or payment proof for verification.

8. Refund Process

Refund requests should be submitted through the official support/WhatsApp channel.

The request should normally include:

  • Registered name
  • Registered email address
  • Purchased product
  • Payment date and time
  • Transaction/payment details
  • Payment screenshot, where applicable
  • Reason for requesting the refund

HPMockShashtra may request additional information where necessary to verify the transaction and assess the refund request.

9. Refund Approval and Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment method or another suitable method available for the transaction, subject to applicable rules, payment-provider requirements and verification requirements.

The time required for the amount to reflect in the user's account may depend on the payment method or payment service provider.

10. Access After Refund

If a refund is approved and processed for a purchased digital service, the corresponding paid access may be cancelled, disabled or revoked.

If the user has already received access but a refund is subsequently approved, HPMockShashtra may revoke the corresponding access after processing the refund.

A user who has received a refund will no longer be entitled to use the paid service associated with that refunded transaction.

11. Cancellation of Purchase

A user may contact HPMockShashtra to request cancellation of a purchase before the purchased digital service has been accessed or used.

Once the purchased digital service has been activated and accessed or attempted, cancellation/refund will generally not be available, except where the refund is otherwise approved under this policy or required by applicable law.

12. Access Expiry

Paid access is provided for the validity period specified for the relevant product at the time of purchase.

Failure to use the service during its validity period does not by itself create a right to a refund or extension.

Once the applicable validity period expires, access may automatically expire in accordance with the product's terms.

13. Fraudulent or Unverified Transactions

HPMockShashtra reserves the right to withhold or delay access where a payment cannot be verified, appears inconsistent with the submitted payment proof, or requires additional verification.

In cases of suspected fraudulent payment activity, access may be withheld or cancelled pending verification.

14. Grievance / Support

For payment, access, refund, cancellation or technical complaints, users may contact HPMockShashtra through the official support contact details provided on the website.

Users are encouraged to provide complete transaction and account information so that the issue can be verified and resolved efficiently.

15. Policy Changes

HPMockShashtra may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time.

Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
