About HPMockShashtra

HPMockShashtra is an online practice platform designed to help aspirants prepare for competitive examinations through mock tests, previous year question papers, and structured practice resources.

Our Purpose

Our aim is to provide a simple and organized platform where aspirants can practice regularly, assess their preparation, identify areas for improvement, and build confidence for their examinations.

What We Offer

Mock Test Series

Full-length practice tests designed for exam-oriented preparation.

Previous Year Question Papers

Resources to understand question patterns and practice previous examination questions.

Performance Tracking

Track attempted tests and monitor your preparation progress.

Study Mode

Review answers, identify mistakes, and understand correct answers after a test.

Hindi + English Support

Bilingual content for convenient and accessible preparation.

Our Approach

Effective preparation is built through three simple steps:

Practice Attempt tests regularly.
Analyse Understand your performance.
Improve Learn from your mistakes.

Our Beginning

HPMockShashtra has been started with a focus on providing preparation resources for competitive examinations in Himachal Pradesh. More examinations, question papers, and study resources may be added over time.
